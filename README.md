# 🎨 Project Concept: Kids' Digital Colouring & NFT App

Inspired by the [Coxygen University](https://coxygen.co/universities/) learning model, I want to create an app that allows kids to take pictures, find pictures to colour in digitally, and mint them as NFTs. These pictures can be accessed on the blockchain, serving as a permanent memory of their childhood, published directly to their parents' wallets.

### Core App Features
- **Kids-Friendly Interface:** A highly attractive, gamified UI where kids accumulate points as they paint digitally.
- **Photo-to-Canvas Converter:** Kids can take photos that instantly transform into black-and-white outlines, ready for digital painting.
- **Educational Games:** Interactive games designed to build mental capacity and understanding of key concepts (detailed below).

---

## 🎮 Game Concepts Aligned with Coxygen's Learning Model

| **Game Concept** | **Coxygen Alignment** | **How It Works (The Fun Part)** | **Cardano Mechanic** | **Placement (Wallet / Web)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. "Fragment Quest"** <br>*(Core Idea)* | **Practice & Build** | A simple H5 puzzle where kids collect "fragments" to build a digital collectible. It reinforces the idea of creating something from parts. | **NFT Minting:** Completing the puzzle triggers a free, real NFT mint on Cardano (testnet for practice, mainnet for real). | **Outside Wallet (Web):** The low-friction entry point from your QR code. The NFT can be sent to the Coxy Wallet later. |
| **2. "Blockchain Builder"** | **Build & Progress** | A drag-and-drop game where kids assemble a "block" from transactions, then link it to the previous block. It visualizes how a chain is formed and why order matters. | **Transaction Simulation:** No real transaction, but teaches the core concept. Could be a prerequisite for unlocking a real micro-transaction in the wallet. | **Inside Wallet:** As an educational module before a child's first real transaction. |
| **3. "Wallet Explorer"** | **Learn & Practice** | A guided, interactive sandbox. Kids are given a "fake" wallet with test ADA and must complete missions (e.g., "Send 1 ADA to your friend," "Mint a token"). It's a safe space to fail. | **Testnet Transactions:** Uses Cardano testnet to simulate real actions without financial risk. Progress is tracked. | **Inside Wallet:** The natural onboarding flow for the Coxy Wallet. |
| **4. "Privacy Protector"** | **Learn & Build** | A game tied to the Midnight & Privacy Tech pathway. Kids must decide what information to share publicly vs. keep private in different scenarios. | **Conceptual:** No on-chain action. It teaches the *why* of privacy before they encounter it in advanced Cardano/Midnight projects. | **Outside Wallet (Web):** A standalone educational game that feeds into the Coxygen curriculum. |
| **5. "NFT Designer"** | **Create & Progress** | A simple drawing/creation tool where kids design their own collectible (e.g., a character or badge). The design is then minted as an NFT. | **NFT Minting:** The creative output becomes a real, on-chain asset. This is a powerful "ownership" moment. | **Outside Wallet (Web):** The creative hook. The NFT can be viewed in the Coxy Wallet. |

### 🧮 Bonus Feature: Math Blocks Game
